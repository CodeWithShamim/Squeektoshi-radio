How Squeektoshi Radio Works

Squeektoshi Radio translates physical proof-of-work telemetry from the WHEELY9000 sensor on Squeekchain into a continuous generative soundscape. Wheel rotations modulate tempo and rhythmic density, block heights establish root keys, and completed rounds trigger harmonic shifts.

Proof Limits & Boundaries: Sensor records indicate accepted magnet passages under configured device thresholds. They verify hardware reporting history and Ethereum settlement, but do not prove biological identity or direction of movement. Read the complete What the system proves documentation.
